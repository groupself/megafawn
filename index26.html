MEGAFAWN // GILGAMESH

The MegaFawn Collective's [ig] "GILGAMESH" will debut at HERE's PUPPETOPIA in April 2027 as an avant-garde retelling of the first epic: the mystic ontology of climate collapse, the origins of patriarchy, queer love & war, writing and the bicameral "sentient" mind.

* Support the Project at HERE (tax deductible!) *

Origin Story

We fell in love with Gilgamesh while teaching it to high schoolers over covid in a Cascadian Forest School and became determined to develop a deep and strange incarnation of the story.

Since then, we have grown the project there and in NYC with the support of the Jim Henson Foundation.

In 2026 we built it up in a residency at Flux Factory and performed fragments, glosses and paratexts of the Epic all up and down NYC's G train in preparation for three sold-out in-progress shows at Future Space in Queens.

We will be debuting tablets I–V at HERE Theater's Puppetopia in April 2027, and are now fundraising for the latter half of the expanded production, with better sets, puppets and production and a threefold increased cast.
